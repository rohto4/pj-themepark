# MORROWLIGHT P6 deep park expansion

Status: active
Started: 2026-08-01 JST
Autonomy window: at least three hours from the user's resume instruction; the original project allows up to 48 hours.

## Corrected objective

The deployed P0–P5 release is the stable baseline. P6 does not reopen MORROWLIGHT's identity; it makes the accepted promise materially truer. The target is not more pages or more effects. It is a park with enough learnable play, spatial continuity, accumulating consequence, authored surprise, sensory response, and replay variation that an extended visit feels intentionally rewarded.

## Baseline failure signatures

- Automated critical routes complete in seconds, so current branch coverage does not demonstrate a rewarding 30–60 minute visit.
- Each major attraction resolves through a small discrete button sequence. Choices diverge, but mastery, anticipation, embodied control, and self-directed discovery remain thin.
- Cross-realm echoes are often textual callouts. They prove state linkage but do not always feel like the park itself changed.
- The Constellary exposes a remembered motif ledger and four ordered buttons; it does not yet deliver the full sky-parade implied by the blueprint.
- Generative audio is technically gesture-gated and deterministic, but no human listening-quality judgment exists.
- Return visits vary by seed and code, but carry-forward curiosity is not yet strong enough to anchor another substantial night.

## P6 capability evals

### E7 — Learn, play, master

At least one hero attraction must contain three legible layers: a safe first response, a relationship the guest can intentionally shape, and an optional mastery/discovery layer. Two different strategies must produce different scene motion, persistent traces, later echoes, and finale behavior. Keyboard/touch/button equivalents must share the same reducer semantics.

### E8 — The park visibly remembers

Given two fixed histories with the same seed, at least three later non-debug surfaces must differ recognizably: living map/environment, another realm or Hushgarden, and the Constellary/keepsake. At least one consequence must be communicated primarily through authored visual behavior rather than explanatory text.

### E9 — Sustained discovery

The deep fixture must expose layered optional discoveries, alternate strategies, cross-realm callbacks, and return-visit changes without score, grind, or exhaustive gating. Automation proves reachability; a separately observed dwell/interaction-density audit must explain what can reward extended attention.

### E10 — Constellary as performance

The finale must unfold through deterministic phases with a visible beginning, development, recognition beat, climax, and release. Earned realm traces must alter geometry, pacing, companions, language, and movement—not only motif labels. Watch-only, active conducting, muted, reduced-motion, and low-power routes remain equivalent in outcome.

### E11 — Sensory causality

Motion, optional sound, visible notation, and environmental response must share high-level events. The guest can identify what their action changed with sound off. No ambient layer may block interaction, exceed the asset budget without a recorded decision, or make low-power a second-class route.

### E12 — A reason to return

A new night must offer seeded variation and at least one authored continuity hook while keeping the experience account-free and local. A returning guest should encounter a meaningful difference within the first attraction, not only in the final code or title.
