# Early naming collision check

Accessed: 2026-08-01 JST  
Status: discovery evidence; not a legal clearance or trademark opinion

## Search boundary

Exact-name web searches were used to avoid beginning the project with an obvious collision in themed entertainment, immersive experiences, or adjacent public brands. This is a practical originality check only.

## Confirmed findings

- `ASTERIA` is already the name of a planned Storyliving by Disney residential community in Chatham Park, North Carolina. Current 2026 coverage describes more than 4,000 planned homes and Disney Imagineering involvement. Source: Builder, “Inside Disney’s Vision for Asteria in North Carolina,” published 2026-07-13, https://www.builderonline.com/land/development/inside-disneys-vision-for-asteria-in-north-carolina
- `Midnight Assembly` is used by an immersive-event company. Source: https://midnightassembly.in/
- `The Museum of Almost` appeared as the title and setting of a publicly accessible 2026 short work. Source: https://medium.com/quirky-rants/the-museum-of-almost-c199fc887e61
- Exact searches for `MORROWLIGHT` plus park/theme-park terms did not surface an existing themed-entertainment project in the checked results. The term does appear as an online contributor/stock-photographer handle, so this is distinctiveness evidence, not exclusivity evidence.
- Exact search for `The Park Between Tomorrows` did not surface a matching public project in the checked results.

## Decision

Reject `ASTERIA` immediately. Use **MORROWLIGHT — The Park Between Tomorrows** as the working identity and replace the collided finale/exhibit names with project-authored compounds. Run a deeper name/domain/trademark check only before paid branding or domain acquisition; neither is currently authorized or required.
