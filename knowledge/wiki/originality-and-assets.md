# Originality and asset provenance

Synthesized: 2026-08-01 JST  
Evidence detail: `knowledge/raw/naming-collision-check.md`

## Accepted rules

- Research contributes abstract principles, constraints, and counterexamples; it does not contribute copied characters, plot, music, attraction layout, world names, promotional phrasing, or distinctive trade dress.
- Project visuals begin from authored SVG geometry, CSS/procedural textures, and project-directed image generation. Generative sound uses original Web Audio synthesis.
- Open fonts or any later public-domain/open asset are self-hosted, version-pinned, license-checked, and recorded in an asset manifest. No asset-library account is required.
- Every shipped non-code asset records creator/method, source or prompt lineage, license where applicable, date, and material edits.
- Search checks are practical collision screens, not legal clearance. No domain purchase or paid branding occurs without separate approval.

## Naming outcome

`ASTERIA` was rejected after discovering the active Storyliving by Disney community name. `MORROWLIGHT — The Park Between Tomorrows` is the working identity because exact checked results did not reveal a themed-entertainment collision. Collided draft names `Midnight Assembly` and `The Museum of Almost` were also replaced.
