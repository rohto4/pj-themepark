# Theme-park experience principles

Synthesized: 2026-08-01 JST  
Evidence detail: `knowledge/raw/theme-park-experience-research.md`

## Confirmed facts used by Morrowlight

- Theme-park delight has been associated not only with surprise but with interest, guest relevance, and goal congruence in one 645-respondent study. [Ma et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annals.2013.02.018)
- Wayfinding research supports landmarks that are visible, distinctive, meaningful, and placed on routes or at decisions; landmark value still depends on task, familiarity, and the person. [Yesiltepe et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10339-021-01012-x)
- An interactive-narrative experiment found higher reported agency when choices appeared to lead to meaningfully different situations. [Cardona-Rivera et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1609/aiide.v10i1.12716)
- More sensory channels are not automatically more memorable; modalities contributed differently and the cited experiment did not show a blanket multimodal advantage. [Willander et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.01681)
- Short breaks show a more reliable average benefit for vigor/fatigue than for task performance; transferring that result to a browser park remains a hypothesis. [Albulescu et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0272460)
- In a complex VR experience, average valence/arousal outperformed peak/end measures for predicting later remembered experience. A finale is not a cure for a weak middle. [Strijbosch et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.01705)

## Project inference

A great browser-native park needs a coherent whole journey: immediate response, legible spatial choice, meaningful consequences, alternating intensity and rest, recognition of earlier actions, and a graceful farewell. “Immersion” is the relationship among those systems, not the volume of animation or sound.

## Accepted recommendations

- Morrowspire is both visual landmark and semantic home; every decision exposes current location, promising next destination, and return route.
- Each material action receives immediate feedback, changes the scene, echoes elsewhere, and/or returns in the finale.
- Loading/travel embellishment is optional, skippable, interruption-safe, and never hides a blocked critical path.
- Hushgarden and calm recaps are structural pacing, not filler.
- The Constellary reflects recognizable traces but is reachable without exhaustive collection, audio, score, or real-time mastery.
- Keyboard, touch, mute, reduced motion, high contrast, and low power are authored routes under the same state consequences.

## Counterevidence retained

Do not optimize for peak/end alone, create decorative choices, disguise loading as busywork, layer senses indiscriminately, rely on an unlabeled landmark, or allow slow opening/state loss/dead ends at the operational edges.

## Uncertainty

These sources largely study physical parks, general wayfinding, work breaks, memory, or interactive narratives. The transfer into Morrowlight must be tested through E1–E5; source support is not guest validation.
